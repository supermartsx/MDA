Password Best Practices

  • Kam az kam 8 characters ka password banayein.
  • Huroof (A-Z, a-z), Numbers (0-9) aur Symbols (@, #, $) ka istemal karein.
  • Password ko kisi unauthorized shakhs ke sath share na karein.
